## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from modern, technical dark-mode interfaces characterized by restraint, analytical precision, and deep structural clarity. Designed for presenting advanced energy transition talent and technical metrics, the UI merges the focused discipline of minimalist AI tooling with high-voltage industrial vitality.

The brand personality is authoritative, frontier-driven, and meticulously engineered. It speaks to enterprise stakeholders, industrial energy consortia, and infrastructure leaders. The visual tone eschews decorative noise in favor of data density, razor-thin structural delimiters, and calibrated bursts of energetic luminescent accents—reflecting clean power generation (solar, offshore and continental wind, green hydrogen, and industrial telemetry).

The aesthetic relies on **minimalist technical modernism** set against deep obsidian foundations. Content hierarchy is communicated through strict typographical cadence, monochromatic foreground balances, and micro-luminance feedback rather than heavy skeuomorphic shading.

## Colors

The palette operates on a base of deep-carbon light absorption, punctuated by spectral accents tied directly to clean technology sectors.

- **Background & Canvas (`#0B0E14`):** A dense carbon base that grounds viewport framing, minimizes visual fatigue, and provides infinite contrast for data readouts.
- **Surfaces & Cards (`#13171F`, `#1A202C`):** Subdued graphite layers that segment content modules without harsh visual fragmentation.
- **Structural Outlines (`#232936`):** Low-contrast structural wireframes providing crisp containment for cards, tables, and inputs.
- **Primary Energy Accent (`#10B981`):** Industrial green representing active generation, solar arrays, and operational health. Reserved for key actions, primary indicators, and verified competency states.
- **Secondary Kinetic Accent (`#06B6D4`):** Atmospheric cyan representing wind kinetics, hydrogen distribution, and automated system telemetry.
- **Foreground Elements (`#F9FAFB`, `#9CA3AF`, `#6B7280`):** Neutral gray and bright white typographic scale calibrated for WCAG AAA contrast ratios on pure graphite backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Geist** for crisp structural reading and primary content with **JetBrains Mono** for telemetry, competency badges, statistics, and metadata tagging.

- **Geist:** Selected for its geometric discipline, compact vertical proportions, and high readability in dark contexts. Headings utilize tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to project an intentional, modern tech-native aesthetic.
- **JetBrains Mono:** Deployed exclusively for contextual indicators, engineering codes, data labels, and technical skill badges. Monospace sizing provides immediate structural contrast against running body text.
- **Metric Scaling:** Quantitative data points use dedicated display weights (`stat-metric`) paired with uppercase monospace units to mimic precision hardware consoles.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an analytical 12-column fluid grid on desktop viewports and scale down to 4-column structures on mobile devices. The visual rhythm emphasizes continuous horizontal containment lines and compact internal spacing, reflecting high data-density command consoles.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid with a maximum container boundary of 1440px. Outer canvas margins are fixed at `2rem`, with standard `1.5rem` gutters between functional modules.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Profile lists collapse from quad-columns into two-column arrangements.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins. Complex engineering dashboards and profile comparison views reflow into stacked vertical streams with horizontal swipe tabs for sub-metrics.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Component interiors prioritize structural symmetry. Input padding and compact card margins use strict 8px increment multiples (`space-sm`, `space-md`, `space-lg`), keeping content density elevated without clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through **tonal stratification and razor-sharp border luminescence**, rejecting traditional blur-heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows.

- **Layer Tiering:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** Deep Carbon `#0B0E14`.
  - **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Graphite `#13171F` delineated by a uniform 1px outline in `#232936`.
  - **Level 2 (Active Modals & Flying Panels):** Elevated Graphite `#1A202C` featuring a heightened outline of `#374151`.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Structural containment is established entirely with precise `1px` borders (`#232936`).
- **Glow & Photon Focus:** In place of shadows, interactive focus states employ targeted micro-glows: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #10B981, 0 0 16px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`. Inactive states remain completely flat.
- **Glass Overlays:** Fixed navigation headers and sticky control bars apply subtle alpha blurring: `background: rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a bottom border of `1px solid #232936`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **disciplined, semi-sharp geometry (Level 1 - Soft)** that echoes physical diagnostic instrumentation and industrial computing terminals.

- **Base Radii:** Standard interactive elements (buttons, text inputs, metric chips) leverage a restrained `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- **Container Radii:** Cards, dashboard panels, and data tables apply `rounded-lg` at `0.5rem` (8px). Large modal envelopes adopt `rounded-xl` at `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Exceptions:** Status beacons and telemetry dot indicators remain strictly circular (`rounded-full`). Pill shapes are prohibited across inputs and action buttons to preserve the architectural, rectilinear aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid high-contrast surface (`#F9FAFB`) with carbon text (`#0B0E14`) or saturated emerald fill (`#10B981`) with dark text for definitive primary actions. 4px corner radius, font weight 500, height 36px/40px.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Graphite surface (`#13171F`) framed by `1px solid #232936`. White text (`#F9FAFB`). Hover state transitions the border to `#374151` with background lightening to `#1A202C`.

### Competency & Verification Chips (Badges)
- Built with `JetBrains Mono` at `11px` (`label-mono-sm`), uppercase with `0.05em` letter tracking.
- Composed of an ultra-subtle tinted background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)` for Solar/Industrial; `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)` for Wind/Hydrogen) alongside a solid 1px border (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` or `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`).
- Preceded by a `5px` luminous circular status dot indicating certified competency levels.

### Graduate & Specialist Cards
- **Architecture:** Graphite background (`#13171F`) bordered by `1px solid #232936`.
- **Header:** Candidate identifier, operational specialization, and geo-readiness tag (e.g., `[LA_GUAJIRA_WIND_ZONE]`).
- **Body:** Grid of quantitative impact metrics (e.g., `MWh Supervised`, `H2 Output Efficiency`) set in `stat-metric`.
- **Footer:** Horizontal cluster of technical competency chips and telemetry indicators.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Background matches canvas or graphite tier (`#0B0E14` or `#13171F`).
- Inactive state utilizes subtle `1px solid #232936`.
- Placeholder text set in muted gray (`#6B7280`).
- Focus state triggers an immediate border shift to `#10B981` paired with a focused 1px perimeter luminescence.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Square-profile indicators (4px radius) with dark borders (`#232936`).
- Checked state fills with `#10B981` featuring an obsidian glyph.
- Segmented tab switchers use unified graphite pill rails containing rectilinear switches.

### Statistical Metric Displays
- Stacked modular components featuring small uppercase monospace captions above bold scalar values (`stat-metric`).
- Accent micro-bars at the top edge of each metric card communicate live capacity and energy yield efficiency.
